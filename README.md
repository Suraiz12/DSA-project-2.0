## PROJECT TOPIC: FINSIGHT DISTRIBUTION & CONSUMER PRODUCTS LTD. — FINANCIAL ANALYTICS CASE STUDY
### Welcome to the FinSight Nigeria Business Analytics Case Study!
This real-world business case is designed for analysts and students mastering Data Analytics, Power BI, Power Query, Data Modelling, DAX, and Financial Reporting.

You are acting as a Senior Business Intelligence Analyst newly engaged by FinSight Distribution & Consumer Products Ltd., a mid-sized Nigerian FMCG distributor operating across major commercial centers in Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Kano, Benin City, Enugu).

### 2. BUSINESS STORYLINE & EXECUTIVE PROBLEM
Over the last two financial years (2024–2025), FinSight Ltd. experienced aggressive topline revenue growth across its wholesale and retail distribution channels. However, during the recent executive board meeting, the Managing Director expressed grave concern:

"Our sales teams are celebrating record revenue numbers, but our bank balances and net operating cash flows tell a completely different story. Revenue is surging, but our profit margins are actively compressing, and net profit before tax is flat to declining! We need to understand exactly why revenue growth is not translating into profit growth."

The company's legacy financial reporting relies on cumbersome, manual Excel workbooks prepared by branch accountants. This process is error-prone, static, and lacks granular visibility into cost drivers.

Management has commissioned you to build an automated, reliable Power BI Financial Reporting Solution that will:
  - Produce a monthly interactive Profit & Loss (Income) [Statement](https://www.microsoft.com)
  - Compare period-over-period financial performance (2024 vs 2025).
  - Pinpoint the major operational and macroeconomic expense drivers behind profit margin compression.
  -  Evaluate branch-level and department-level cost efficiencies.
  - Provide data-backed actionable strategic recommendations for executive leadership.
